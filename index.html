I like taking a messy, imbalanced dataset and a rough interface idea and turning both into something that actually works — a model that predicts cleanly, and a screen that's a pleasure to use.


I'm a Data Science & Python developer pursuing an MSc in Health Data Science at the University of Aberdeen. My work sits at the intersection of machine learning and design: I build models — from fraud detection and credit risk management to scalable healthcare platforms — and I've also led design for large-scale events, pairing deep analytical work with hands-on UI/UX design to turn raw data and rough ideas into clean, usable products.


Day to day I work in Python, Pandas, NumPy, and Scikit-learn for analysis and modeling, and HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for the front end, with Figma and Adobe Illustrator for everything visual. I care about making the analytical side of a project (the model, the pipeline, the numbers) just as accessible and well-presented as the interface people actually see. You can explore my code at github.com/Dokja-pvt.
